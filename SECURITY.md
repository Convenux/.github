# Security policy

If you've found a vulnerability in Convenux, the iPhone or Android app, convenux.com,
db.convenux.com or their APIs, please tell us privately.

Email [hello@convenux.com](mailto:hello@convenux.com) with "Security" in the subject line.
Describe what you found, how to reproduce it, and what someone could do with it. Please
don't post details publicly until we've fixed it.

We'll confirm we've received your report, keep you updated while we work on it, and
credit you when it's fixed if you'd like.

Please don't access or change other people's data beyond what you need to show the
problem, and don't run denial-of-service, spam or social-engineering tests.
